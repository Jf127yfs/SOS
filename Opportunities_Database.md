- **Deadline:** July 20, 2026, 5:00 PM EDT (14 days remaining)

- Equipment must support maritime industry training

**Target Focus:**

- Shipbuilding and vessel repair training
- Equipment purchases for hands-on training
- Instructor hiring/compensation

**Required Equipment Examples:**

- Simulation systems

1. Verify Michigan Community College eligibility
2. Prepare equipment list with justifications
3. Detail instructor hiring plan (if applicable)

5. Email submission to: LEO-Industry-Engagement@michigan.gov
